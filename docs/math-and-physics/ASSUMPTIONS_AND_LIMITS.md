# Assumptions, evidence and validity limits

## What the implementation supports

This is an empirical curve-fitting and teaching package. Three bounded-parameter families can summarize a supplied capacity trace; chronological validation compares forward prediction on later observations from that trace; EOL/RUL is conditional on the chosen family, threshold and bounded extrapolation rule. Finite outputs and passed software tests do not establish chemistry calibration, operational safety or out-of-domain predictive accuracy. [bcla/core.py:18–42](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/bcla/core.py#L18-L42) [bcla/core.py:299–435](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/bcla/core.py#L299-L435)

There is no electrochemical state, spatial thermal field, calendar-aging law, rainflow model, online estimator, pack balancing logic, SOC safety constraint or degradation coupling to the preserved duty-cycle metadata in the native fitter. The Arrhenius helper and temperature plot are separate illustrations. The source gives an empirical intercept, not measured physical initial/boundary conditions for an ODE or PDE. [bcla/core.py:179–241](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/bcla/core.py#L179-L241) [bcla/core.py:539–566](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/bcla/core.py#L539-L566) [bcla/viz.py:153–201](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/bcla/viz.py#L153-L201) [bcla/datasets.py:746–923](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/bcla/datasets.py#L746-L923)

## Data and statistical assumptions

- **Synthetic fixtures:** LFP/NMC labels identify seeded formulas with additive Gaussian noise and clipping; they do not constitute validated chemistry benchmarks. CSV examples are schema/demo data. [bcla/datasets.py:17–71](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/bcla/datasets.py#L17-L71) [README.md:185–188](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/README.md#L185-L188) [data/cycle_metadata_example.csv:1–7](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/data/cycle_metadata_example.csv#L1-L7)
- **Observation scale:** capacity normalization is a loader choice, not a fitter operation. The simple and long-form loaders use different reference observations. Preserve raw units, reference capacity and measurement protocol. [bcla/datasets.py:653–743](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/bcla/datasets.py#L653-L743) [bcla/datasets.py:893–923](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/bcla/datasets.py#L893-L923)
- **Holdout scope:** one chronological split within supplied observations neither groups multiple cells nor tests a genuinely independent final dataset. The model-selection score and full-data fit have different training histories. [bcla/core.py:277–404](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/bcla/core.py#L277-L404)
- **Residual bootstrap:** resampling centered residuals assumes their exchangeability for this analysis. Serial dependence, heteroscedasticity, model-family uncertainty and protocol/measurement uncertainty require separate treatment. Bounds are withheld after any censored or failed replicate. [bcla/core.py:407–533](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/bcla/core.py#L407-L533)
- **Projection:** the EOL window is a computational policy, not a demonstrated validity range. A `None` estimate means no supported crossing was returned. Direct `project` can extrapolate farther and return negative capacity. [bcla/core.py:70–111](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/bcla/core.py#L70-L111)
- **Timestamp/throughput convention:** metadata is not automatically aligned to model parameters. EFC integrates the interpolant of clipped current samples, and Oxford preprocessing repairs/counts irregular times under explicit rules. [bcla/datasets.py:108–203](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/bcla/datasets.py#L108-L203) [examples/oxford_energy_trading.py:123–247](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/examples/oxford_energy_trading.py#L123-L247)

The Oxford adapter identifies an external measured dataset and its source-declared licensing, but this review downloaded no data. Its dated 2026-08-31 audit is preserved as a historical report. Offline fixture tests, native synthetic examples and current source inspection do not independently reproduce the measured-data table or establish present archive availability. [docs/oxford-energy-trading.md:9–25](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/docs/oxford-energy-trading.md#L9-L25) [docs/oxford-energy-trading.md:83–112](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/docs/oxford-energy-trading.md#L83-L112) [tests/test_oxford_live.py:1–48](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/tests/test_oxford_live.py#L1-L48)

## Software and presentation limits

The tested local environment was Python 3.12, not all declared supported versions or every possible dependency resolution. No complete native security scanner rebuild, vulnerability-database refresh, live PyPI installation, browser interaction, screen-reader audit or published deployment verification was performed by the author. The existing configured gates remain separate integration requirements. [Verification ledger](../maintainer/VERIFICATION_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md).

The browser uses a fixed synthetic 1000-cycle exponential proxy with its own temperature/rate formula, resistance-growth proxy and illustrative throughput. It does not ingest the native fitted parameters, data files or confidence intervals. Its eight passed checkpoint fixtures establish agreement with that implemented proxy at stated inputs. They do not validate field behavior or the nine other bundled model kinds. [docs/index.html:155–189](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/docs/index.html#L155-L189) [docs/index.html:269–273](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/docs/index.html#L269-L273) [docs/index.html:469–491](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/blob/02ed161bded286dad4b3624961430a564715c782/docs/index.html#L469-L491)

Before expanding scientific claims, specify the intended population/protocol, coordinate definition, normalization, admissible domain, independent validation data, error metrics and uncertainty sources. The [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) makes those proposed studies explicit; this documentation change does not implement them.
