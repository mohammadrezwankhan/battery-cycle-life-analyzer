## Pages publication and rollback

The workflow uploads the entire `docs/` directory; it has no Jekyll build step despite the retained `docs/_config.yml`. Therefore `docs/reference/index.html` must already be regenerated in the reviewed commit. After deployment, inspect `/battery-cycle-life-analyzer/reference/`, its visible source/date, internal anchors and navigation from all three existing surfaces. Trace the deployment to the actual merged SHA. The author has not verified this candidate live.
